**Moderation/block/deletion separation:** P03-3's internal restriction helper is **not** an authenticated staff endpoint; an authorized staff workflow must call it after verifying privileges and policy. `uluv_blocks` is viewer-specific, so P03-4 checks both directions on every candidate hydration; an individual block does not globally remove a person's pointer when a viewer blocks them. Full account deletion (erasure of identities, media, posts etc.), appeals, reinstatement and incident repair of unanchored orphan keys require separate approved workflows, not accidental code added to this private-pointer phase. They are not claimed implemented by P03-3.

**Postal PIN code lookup remains on hold.** Requires separately licensed/verified government India Post multiple-office data; GeoNames does not prove PIN ownership, and no PIN may substitute for trusted selected approximate locality. No PIN→GPS guessing or unverified search UI.

S3/CloudFront owner-managed DEV data and approved source hashes only. Never send credentials or precise coordinates to CDN. P01/P02 session, photo privacy, guided onboarding, two/five-tab navigation and accepted private H3 budgets remain unchanged. No hidden ranking, paid prioritization, constant location tracking or mock-only working buttons.
